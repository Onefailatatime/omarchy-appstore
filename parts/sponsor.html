Fair by design

How the rotation works

  1. Three at a time

    The header and footer each show three sponsors at once, rotating through every active sponsor in order. App banners don't rotate, they're yours alone on your app's page.

  2. Equal screen time

    The rotation advances on a fixed timer and every active sponsor gets exactly the same visibility. There's no bidding for position, ever, the order is the order you signed up.

  3. Contracts keep it honest

    Your sponsor unit appears only inside your 90 day contract dates and drops out automatically when the term ends. The starting position changes daily, so nobody is permanently first or last.

House rules

What a sponsor unit is

  • A name, one line of text, a link, and an optional 300 by 180 image. No scripts, no tracking pixels, every unit is static HTML with a visible tag, and links carry rel="sponsored".
  • Software, hardware, hosting, and tools that belong next to a Linux package directory. Sponsors unrelated to the audience get declined with a full refund.
  • No editorial say. Sponsoring never affects a package's listing, ranking, or review, those come from the official repo and reader votes.
  • You own your banner image. Send a 300 by 180 PNG or SVG, or just a logo, we'll set the unit type.

Terms

What a sponsorship means

  • 90 day terms that renew. Sponsor spots run in 90 day terms from the day you subscribe and renew automatically. Cancel any time from the Stripe receipt email; your sponsor unit runs to the end of the term.
  • Unlimited spots. No bidding, no position auctions. As many sponsors as sign up share the rotation equally.
  • Live within one business day. Once your sponsorship is confirmed, send the name, one line, link, and optional image, and your sponsor unit goes up.
  • Your details stay private. We publish your sponsor unit, not your invoice. Sponsorship terms are never disclosed to other sponsors.
