Fair by design

How the rotation works

  1. Three at a time

    The header and footer each show three sponsors at once, rotating through every active sponsor in order. App banners don't rotate — they're yours alone on your app's page.

  2. Equal screen time

    The rotation advances on a fixed timer and every active sponsor gets exactly the same visibility. There's no bidding for position, ever — the order is the order you signed up.

  3. Contracts keep it honest

    Your ad appears only inside your 90-day contract dates and drops out automatically when the term ends. The starting position changes daily, so nobody is permanently first or last.

House rules

What a sponsor unit is

  • A name, one line of text, a link, and an optional 300×180 image. No scripts, no tracking pixels — every unit is static HTML with a visible tag, and links carry rel="sponsored".
  • Software, hardware, hosting, and tools that belong next to a Linux package directory. Ads unrelated to the audience get declined with a full refund.
  • No editorial say. Sponsoring never affects a package's listing, ranking, or review — those come from the official repo and reader votes.
  • You own your banner image. Send a 300×180 PNG or SVG, or just a logo — we'll set the unit type.

Terms

What a sponsorship means

  • 90-day terms. Every sponsorship runs 90 days from the day it goes live, billed up front as a single payment (monthly rate × 3).
  • Flat rates, unlimited spots. The price on the card is the price — no bidding, no position auctions. As many sponsors as sign up share the rotation equally.
  • Invoices are due within 3 days. Your ad goes live once payment lands; the contract clock starts that day.
  • Your details stay private. We publish your ad, not your invoice. Sponsor names and rates are never disclosed to other sponsors.
