Three steps

How it works

  1. Place a bid

    Pick a slot above, enter your amount, product, link, and email. Each bid is checked before it joins the board, usually within a day.

  2. Highest bid holds it

    The clock runs from the first approved bid. When it ends, the winner gets an invoice by email and the ad goes live when it is paid.

  3. Edit any time

    New link, text, or image whenever you like. Free. The position never moves.

Sold once, never again

The lifetime slot

  • Timed auction. The first bid at or above the reserve starts a __AUCTION_DAYS__ day clock. Outbid each other until it runs out.
  • No sniping. A bid in the last __EXTEND_HOURS__ hours adds __EXTEND_HOURS__ hours.
  • One payment. The winner pays once and keeps the spot for the life of the site. No renewal, no rate change, nobody can outbid you after close.
  • Outbid, owe nothing. Only the winning bid is invoiced. Term slots run the same way on a __TERM_AUCTION_DAYS__ day clock.

House rules

What an ad can be

  • A name, one line, a link, and for sidebar units an optional 300 by 180 image. Set in the site's own type and colours, always tagged Ad.
  • No scripts or tracking. Links carry rel="sponsored".
  • Software, hardware, hosting, and tools that belong next to a Linux package directory. No gambling, adult, or token promotion.
  • Any ad can be declined with a refund. Sponsoring never changes a listing, a rank, or a vote.

Bidding terms

What a bid means

  • A bid is a commitment. If it wins, you agree to pay the amount you entered: once for the lifetime slot, or for the first 90 day term on a paid slot. Nothing is charged for a bid that does not win.
  • Bids are checked before they count. A bid joins the board once it is reviewed, usually within a day. Bids that look fake or that break the house rules are removed.
  • Invoices are due within 3 days. Unpaid, the slot passes to the next highest bid.
  • Your details stay private. Only amounts are public. Your name, link, and email are used to run the auction and to invoice you, nothing else.
