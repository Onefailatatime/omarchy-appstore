Advertise on Omarchy Apps

Four sponsor slots, sold by open bid. One of them is yours for life.

See the slots and bid ↓

The store is read by people setting up Omarchy and choosing what to install. It carries no tracking and no ad network. Instead it sells four fixed slots directly, and the price is whatever the market says it is: you bid, the highest bid holds the slot.

Three steps

How bidding works

  1. Pick a slot, send a bid

    Each slot has a reserve, the lowest bid it accepts. Send your bid and your creative by DM on X. The current high bid is published on this page.

  2. Highest bid wins the term

    Paid slots run in 90 day terms. When a term opens, the highest bid holds it. You get an invoice, and the ad goes live once it is paid.

  3. Change the creative any time

    Send a new link, text, or image whenever you like, at no charge. It is rebuilt into the page within a day. The slot and position never change.

Sold once, never again

The lifetime sponsor slot

One slot on the site is sold once and never again. It sits at the top of the right rail beside the Top 3 podium on desktop and directly under the search bar on phones, so it is above the fold on every screen. It carries a "Lifetime sponsor" tag and a double border, and nothing else ever takes that spot.

  • Open auction. Any bid at or above the reserve starts a __AUCTION_DAYS__ day clock. Anyone can outbid until the clock runs out. The close date is published here the moment the clock starts.
  • Anti-sniping. A bid in the final __EXTEND_HOURS__ hours extends the auction by __EXTEND_HOURS__ hours, so it ends when bidding stops, not when a timer does.
  • One payment, no renewal. The winner pays once. The ad stays for as long as the site exists, in the same position, with no term and no rate change.
  • Edit freely. The sponsor can change the website, link, text, and image at any time, as often as they like.
  • Outbid, owe nothing. Only the winning bid is invoiced. If you are outbid you pay nothing.

Four positions

Slots and current bids

Reserves are per month for the 90 day slots and are billed per term. Bids above the reserve are welcome and expected. Figures update at each site build.

House rules

What an ad can be

  • A product name, one line of text, a link, and for the rail and lifetime units an optional 300 by 180 image. Rendered in the site's own type and colours so it reads as part of the page, with a visible "Ad" tag so nobody mistakes it for a listing.
  • No scripts, pixels, or tracking of any kind. Links carry rel="sponsored". The site's content policy stays exactly as strict as it is today.
  • Software, hardware, hosting, services, and tools that make sense next to a Linux package directory. No gambling, adult content, crypto token promotion, or anything that would mislead a reader.
  • Any ad can be declined or removed at the store's discretion. In that case the unused part of the term is refunded, and a lifetime payment is refunded in full.
  • Sponsorship buys the slot, not the listings. Package cards, rankings, votes, and categories are never influenced by who is sponsoring.
